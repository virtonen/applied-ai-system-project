# Vibe words

## study
Related words: homework, studying, revision, reading
Study music is steady and calm so you can concentrate.

## cozy
Related words: snug, comfy, homey, hygge
Cozy music feels warm and safe, like being indoors on a cold day.

## rainy
Related words: rain, drizzle, wet, showers
Rainy music is soft and reflective, like a grey day indoors.

## nocturnal
Related words: night, midnight, evening, nighttime
Nocturnal music fits late hours when the world is quiet.

## driving
Related words: drive, road, commute, roadtrip
Driving music has a steady forward pulse for the road.

## workout
Related words: gym, exercise, running, training, cardio
Workout music is loud, fast and built to keep you moving.

## calm
Related words: still, quiet, soothing
Calm music is slow and low in energy.

## summery
Related words: summer, beach, holiday, vacation
Summery music is bright and sunny, made for warm days.

## sunny
Related words: bright, cheerful, golden
Sunny music has a warm, happy glow.

## dreamy
Related words: floaty, hazy, drifting, wistful
Dreamy music floats and drifts, a little out of focus.

## spacey
Related words: cosmic, space, galaxy, celestial
Spacey music sounds wide and weightless.

## retro
Related words: vintage, eighties, oldschool
Retro music borrows the sound of an earlier decade.

## gritty
Related words: rough, street, grimy
Gritty music is raw and rough around the edges.

## powerful
Related words: epic, mighty, loud
Powerful music is big, strong and hard to ignore.

## carefree
Related words: breezy, easygoing, lighthearted
Carefree music is light and has no worries.

## warm
Related words: friendly, gentle, mellow
Warm music is soft and welcoming.
