# Moods

## happy
Related words: cheerful, joyful, glad, merry
Happy music feels cheerful and carefree.

## chill
Related words: mellow, unwind, lounge, easygoing
Chill music is easygoing and low-pressure.

## intense
Related words: fierce, strong, hardcore
Intense music is driving and powerful, and it keeps your energy up.

## relaxed
Related words: relaxing, calm, mellow, soothing, laidback
Relaxed music is gentle and unhurried, for winding down.

## moody
Related words: brooding, pensive, shadowy
Moody music feels dark and thoughtful, like a late evening.

## focused
Related words: concentration, concentrate, attentive
Focused music is steady and unobtrusive so it stays out of the way of work.

## defiant
Related words: rebellious, bold, unapologetic
Defiant music is confident and pushes back.

## nostalgic
Related words: wistful, throwback, sentimental
Nostalgic music looks back warmly at the past.

## melancholy
Related words: sad, blue, gloomy, somber, tearful
Melancholy music is slow and sorrowful, with a quiet sadness.

## aggressive
Related words: angry, furious, harsh, rage
Aggressive music is loud and hard-hitting.

## uplifting
Related words: inspiring, hopeful, positive, encouraging
Uplifting music lifts your mood and feels hopeful.

## romantic
Related words: love, tender, intimate, date
Romantic music is tender and warm, and it is often about love.

## euphoric
Related words: ecstatic, blissful, elated
Euphoric music feels like a huge, joyful rush.

## peaceful
Related words: tranquil, serene, quiet, gentle
Peaceful music is quiet and still, with nothing to rush you.
