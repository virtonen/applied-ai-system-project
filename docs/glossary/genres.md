# Genres

## pop
Related words: mainstream, catchy, radio, chart
Pop is catchy, polished music with big hooks that is made to be sung along to.

## lofi
Related words: lo, chillhop, beats
Lofi is mellow, slightly worn-sounding beats, often instrumental, that people play while studying or relaxing.

## rock
Related words: guitar, band, riffs
Rock is guitar-driven music with a strong beat and a live-band feel.

## ambient
Related words: atmospheric, soundscape, drone
Ambient is slow, spacious sound that sets a mood instead of telling a story.

## jazz
Related words: jazzy, swing, bebop, improvised
Jazz is improvised, swinging music built on rich chords and usually played by small bands.

## synthwave
Related words: retrowave, outrun, synths
Synthwave is retro electronic music that sounds like an old science fiction film soundtrack.

## indie pop
Related words: indie, alternative
Indie pop is pop with a handmade, independent feel that is often lighter and more personal than mainstream pop.

## hip hop
Related words: rap, rapper, bars, hiphop
Hip hop is rhythm-led music built around rapped or spoken lyrics over a strong beat.

## country
Related words: twang, western, nashville
Country is story-driven music with warm guitars about home, roads and heartache.

## classical
Related words: orchestral, symphony, orchestra
Classical is composed instrumental music for orchestras or soloists that is often slow and expressive.

## metal
Related words: heavy, headbanging, distorted
Metal is loud, fast, heavily distorted guitar music with an aggressive edge.

## reggae
Related words: island, jamaican, skank
Reggae is relaxed music with offbeat rhythms from Jamaica and a sunny, laid-back feel.

## r&b
Related words: rnb, soul, groove
R&B is smooth, soulful music with a steady groove that is often about love.

## edm
Related words: electronic, dance, club, rave
EDM is electronic dance music made for clubs and festivals, with big builds and drops.

## folk
Related words: traditional, singer, songwriter
Folk is simple, acoustic music that tells stories, usually with a gentle voice and a guitar.
